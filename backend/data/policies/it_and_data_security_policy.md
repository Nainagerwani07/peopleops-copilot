# IT and Data Security Policy

## Company Devices
Every employee gets a company laptop. Company laptops must not be shared with anyone outside NovaWorks, including family members. Sensitive company data must be accessed only on approved devices; personal devices may be used only for email and chat, through the company's mobile management app.

## Passwords and Access
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and are changed every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, CB Nest and source code. Never share passwords, one-time codes or access tokens with anyone, including IT staff.

## Information Classification
All documents must be labelled Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Employee personal data such as salary, bank details, PAN and date of birth is Restricted and may only be accessed by HR and Finance for their work.

## Reporting Security Incidents
Security incidents, such as a lost laptop, a phishing email that was clicked, or data sent to the wrong person, must be reported to IT within 1 hour of being noticed, by raising an HR ticket with the category IT Security or calling the IT helpdesk.

## Onboarding Access
New joiners receive their laptop and system access within two business days of joining. Access to additional systems needs approval from the reporting manager.
