# Holiday and Comp Off Policy

## Public Holidays
NovaWorks observes 10 fixed public holidays each year. The list is published in the HR holiday calendar in CB Nest every December for the following year.

## Optional Holidays
In addition, employees can choose 2 optional holidays a year from the optional holiday list, for festivals not covered by the fixed holidays. Optional holidays must be applied for like casual leave and cannot be carried forward.

## Working on a Holiday or Weekend
Working on a public holiday or weekend requires prior approval from the reporting manager. Overtime work on a working day also requires prior manager acknowledgement and is not paid separately.

## Comp Off
A full day worked on an approved holiday or weekend earns 1 comp off day; at least 4 hours earns half a day. Comp off must be applied for in CB Nest and approved by the manager.
Comp off requests are valid for 60 days from the date the comp off was earned. Unused comp off lapses after 60 days and is not encashed.
