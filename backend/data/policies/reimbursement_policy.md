# Reimbursement Policy

## Travel Reimbursement
Business travel must be pre-approved by the reporting manager. Travel claims must be submitted within 15 days of return.
Economy class is allowed for flights under 6 hours. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 per night elsewhere. A daily meal allowance of INR 1,200 is paid during travel, with no receipts needed.

## Business Expenses
Business expenses other than travel, such as client meals, software subscriptions and training fees, require prior manager approval and must be claimed within 30 days of the expense. Client meals are reimbursed up to INR 2,500 per person.

## Claim Submission and Approval
Claims are submitted as an HR ticket in CB Nest with the category Reimbursement. Every claim must include a valid invoice or receipt showing the vendor name, date, amount and tax details; card statements or payment screenshots alone are not accepted as proof. Claims of up to INR 5,000 are approved by the reporting manager alone. Claims above INR 5,000 and up to INR 50,000 need approval from both the reporting manager and the department head. Claims above INR 50,000 additionally need approval from the Finance team. A manager cannot approve their own claim; it goes to their own manager. Once fully approved, claims are paid with the next monthly salary if approved before the 20th of the month, otherwise with the salary of the following month. Claims submitted after the deadline (15 days for travel, 30 days for other expenses) are rejected unless the department head grants an exception in writing. Rejected claims can be resubmitted once, within 7 days of rejection, with the missing documents. Claims in a foreign currency are converted to INR at the exchange rate on the date of the expense, and card conversion fees are reimbursed if the card statement is attached. Duplicate or altered receipts are treated as misconduct under the Code of Conduct, and the full amount may be recovered from salary.

## Not Reimbursable
Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, upgrades to business class not approved in advance, and expenses for family members travelling with the employee are not reimbursed.
