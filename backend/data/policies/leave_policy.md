# Leave Policy

Applies to all full-time employees of NovaWorks. Interns and contractors follow the terms in their contract.

## Leave Year
The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Balances are credited monthly and shown on the Leaves page in CB Nest.

## Earned Leave
Every full-time employee earns 1.5 days of earned leave per month, up to 18 days a year.
Unused earned leave can be carried forward to the next year, up to a maximum of 30 days. Any balance above 30 days lapses on 31 December.
Earned leave must be applied for at least 7 days in advance. Earned leave cannot be taken during the notice period without HR approval.

## Casual Leave
Employees get 8 days of casual leave per year for short personal needs. Casual leave can be applied for on the same day, but not more than 2 consecutive days at a time. Casual leave cannot be carried forward.

## Sick Leave
Employees get 12 days of sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.
For sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days, a medical certificate must be uploaded within 3 working days of returning to work.

## Parental Leave
Birthing parents get 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Non-birthing parents get 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be used within 3 months of the birth or adoption. Parental leave must be informed to the manager and HR at least 4 weeks in advance where possible.

## Approval
All leave requests are approved by the reporting manager. A manager cannot approve their own leave; it goes to HR instead.
Leave taken without approval is treated as loss of pay (LOP). Leave that is rejected after it was taken is also marked as LOP.

## Leave Encashment
At separation, up to 30 days of unused earned leave are paid out at the basic salary rate. Casual and sick leave are not encashed.
