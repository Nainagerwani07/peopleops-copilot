# Attendance Policy

## Working Hours
The standard working day is 9 hours including a 1-hour lunch break, Monday to Friday. Core hours are 10:30 AM to 4:30 PM, when everyone is expected to be available.

## Clock-In and Clock-Out
Employees must clock in on CB Nest before 9:30 AM on working days and clock out at the end of the day. Working from home also requires clocking in.

## Late Marks
A clock-in after 9:30 AM is recorded as a late mark. Three late marks in a calendar month count as half a day of casual leave. If no casual leave is left, the half day is treated as loss of pay.

## Missed Punches and Regularization
If an employee forgets to clock in or out, they can raise a regularization request in CB Nest within 3 working days. The reporting manager approves the request. More than 4 regularization requests in a month are reviewed by HR.

## Absence Without Notice
Being absent for 3 consecutive working days without informing the manager or HR is treated as unauthorized absence and may lead to disciplinary action under the Code of Conduct.
