# Code of Conduct

## Professional Behaviour
All employees must treat colleagues, clients and vendors with respect. Harassment, discrimination and bullying of any kind are not tolerated. Official communication must use company-approved channels.

## Dress Code
Business casual is expected in the office from Monday to Thursday. Fridays are casual. Formal business attire is required for client meetings, unless the client has a different dress code.

## Conflicts of Interest
Employees must declare to HR any outside job, business or close relationship that could conflict with their work at NovaWorks. Gifts from vendors or clients worth more than INR 3,000 must be declared and may need to be returned.

## Raising a Grievance
Employees can raise a grievance through an HR ticket in CB Nest with the category Grievance. HR acknowledges grievances within 2 working days and aims to resolve them within 15 working days. Complaints of sexual harassment go to the Internal Committee (IC), which handles them confidentially.

## Disciplinary Action
Breaking this code can lead to a verbal warning, a written warning, suspension or termination, depending on how serious the breach is. Fraud, harassment and data theft can lead to immediate termination.
