# Work From Home Policy

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed probation can work from home. Employees on probation can work from home only with written approval from their manager and HR.

## Allowance
Eligible employees can work from home up to 2 days a week. Unused WFH days do not carry over to the next week. Fully remote arrangements need approval from the department head and HR.

## Requesting WFH
Planned WFH must be requested in CB Nest at least 1 working day in advance and approved by the reporting manager. Same-day WFH for emergencies is allowed, but the manager must be informed before 9:30 AM.

## Expectations While Working From Home
Employees must clock in, be reachable on company-approved channels during core hours (10:30 AM to 4:30 PM), and attend scheduled meetings with video on when asked. Company data must only be accessed from approved devices, as described in the IT and Data Security Policy.

## Home Office Allowance
Employees who have completed one year of service can claim a one-time home office allowance of INR 10,000 for a chair, desk or monitor, through the Reimbursement Policy process.
