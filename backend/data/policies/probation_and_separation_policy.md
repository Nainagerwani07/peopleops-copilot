# Probation and Separation Policy

## Probation
New employees are on probation for six months from their joining date, unless the offer letter explicitly waives it. The manager can extend probation once, by up to three months, with a written reason. Probation is confirmed in writing by HR.

## Notice Period
The notice period is 30 days during probation and 60 days after confirmation. The company may agree to an earlier last working day, or pay salary in lieu of notice. Earned leave cannot be used to shorten the notice period without HR approval.

## Resignation
Employees resign by submitting a resignation request to their reporting manager and HR. The manager has 3 working days to discuss it with the employee before HR confirms the last working day.

## Exit Clearance
Before the last working day, the employee must return the laptop, ID card and any other company assets, and hand over their work. IT disables system access at the end of the last working day.
Exit clearance must be complete before the final settlement is processed.

## Final Settlement
The final settlement includes salary up to the last working day, encashment of unused earned leave as described in the Leave Policy, and approved reimbursements, minus any recoveries. It is paid within 45 days of the last working day. The relieving letter and experience letter are issued along with the settlement.
